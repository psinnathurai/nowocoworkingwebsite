About nowo

Coworking, built for Widnau.

nowo is the first and only coworking space in Widnau. We offer 16 desks for freelancers, remote workers and small teams, with access around the clock. Less commuting, more focus, and a community right on your doorstep.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How much does coworking in Widnau cost?

At nowo a day pass costs CHF 35, a half-day pass CHF 20 and a Flex Desk CHF 300 per month. A dedicated desk (Fix Desk) costs CHF 450 per month, part-time plans start at CHF 125 per month. All prices exclude VAT; monthly plans have a minimum term of one month, annual plans one year.

Are there day and half-day passes?

Yes, the day pass costs CHF 35 and the half-day pass CHF 20. Both are one-offs with no commitment, coffee and Wi-Fi included.

Can I work around the clock?

Yes, members have 24/7 access. You unlock the door with the KleverKey app on your phone, no key or badge needed.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is available at nowo, Bahnhofstrasse 14 in Widnau.

How do I get there by public transport?

Take the train to Heerbrugg station; from there it is a 7 to 10 minute walk to Bahnhofstrasse 14.

Can I rent a meeting room by the hour?

Yes, the meeting room costs CHF 35 per hour, CHF 30 per hour from 4 hours and CHF 25 per hour from 8 hours. It offers 31 m², 12 chairs, a camera for video calls, a 65-inch screen with Miracast and free coffee. Once registered, you book it in the member portal.

Can I rent a business address (virtual office)?

Yes, the Virtual Office costs CHF 100 per month and includes a domicile address in Widnau with mail handling.

Can I come by and try it out?

Yes, the easiest way is a day pass for CHF 35 or a half-day pass for CHF 20. Beforehand you can take the 360° tour online or write to us for a tour in person.

Who is nowo for?

nowo is for freelancers, remote workers and small teams in the Rhine Valley who want to work close to home instead of commuting to Zurich or St. Gallen. Fast internet, height-adjustable desks, monitors, a printer and coffee are included.

How do I book?

You book online at booking.nowo.space: pick a membership or a day pass and finish in a few clicks.
