About nowo

Coworking, built for Widnau.

nowo is the first and only coworking space in Widnau. We offer 16 desks for freelancers, remote workers and small teams, with access around the clock. Less commuting, more focus, and a community right on your doorstep.

I work in IT, in consulting and engineering. My last few roles were in Zurich, Olten and Bern, but for family, for love, and because I belong to the Rhine Valley, I came back. nowo grew out of that: a place to work right here, without commuting every day.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How do I get in?

Once you're registered, the KleverKey app on your phone unlocks the door any time, 24/7, no key or badge needed.

How do I book a desk?

Through our platform, booking.nowo.space. Pick a membership or a day pass and book in a few clicks.

Is there a minimum term?

A monthly membership has a minimum term of one month, an annual membership one year. Day and half-day passes are one-offs with no commitment.

What's included?

Fast internet, height-adjustable desks, monitors, a printer, meeting-room use and coffee.

Can I get a business address?

Yes, the Virtual Office at CHF 100 per month gives you a domicile address in Widnau including mail handling.

Can I see the space first?

Of course. Take the 360° tour online, or write to us for a tour in person.
